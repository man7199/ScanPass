# ScanPass

**Engine version:** Unreal Engine 5.8

## About the game

ScanPass is a focused training game for player scanning and possession play.

You play in first person on a full-size football pitch. Your team-mates and opponents move into new positions after every pass. Before the ball reaches you, glance left and right to see who is free (hold Q / E). Then hold the left mouse button to charge a pass and release to kick. An aim ring shows where the ball will stop. Find the free team-mate, keep possession and complete 10 passes to reach the next level. Each misplaced pass costs one of your three lives.

There are five levels, from Beginner to Championship. The last level is endless. The opponents press harder as you go up. The game is available in English and Japanese.

## Features

- Procedural 3D grass (custom `ProceduralGrass` plugin): millions of individual blades generated on the GPU, with mowing stripes, wind, and distance-based LOD. Blades bend around the ball and the players.
- Ball physics tuned for real grass: realistic ground passes, rolling resistance and bounces.
- A scanning mechanic that trains you to check your surroundings before you receive the ball.
- Increasing difficulty
